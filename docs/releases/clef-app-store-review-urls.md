Clef & Staff is presented as a separate app from the in C classical discovery
surface, with the Android package `com.mannlab.clef` and iOS bundle
`com.mannlab.inc.clefandstaff`.

Privacy summary for review:

- The app is local-first and does not require an account.
- Imported PDFs, annotations, metadata, setlists, bookmarks, and app settings
  stay on the user's device unless the user exports or backs them up through
  the operating system or another service.
- Microphone access is used only for the chromatic tuner. Audio is processed on
  device and is not stored as recordings or sent to mann-lab-apps servers.
- File access is used for user-initiated PDF import and sharing.
- The app does not use location, contacts, advertising tracking, or third-party
  ad SDKs.
